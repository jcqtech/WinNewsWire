Subscribe to Feed

Open subscriptions in

Using the toolbar button

Open a website or feed, then click Subscribe to Feed. Approve Edge's request to open the desktop reader. Your original page stays open; Edge may leave a blank handoff tab.

WinNewsWire discovers feeds from the page address and opens its subscription dialog. A page does not have to be a direct RSS, Atom, or JSON Feed URL.

If the reader does not open

Install the packaged version of WinNewsWire so Windows registers its x-winnewswire-feed: protocol. For the default-reader option, choose an app for the FEED link type in Windows Settings > Apps > Default apps.

Edge or Windows may ask which app to open. Canceling that prompt does not subscribe. The extension cannot detect cancellation, a missing protocol handler, or whether the reader found a feed. Managed-browser policies may also block external applications.

Only HTTP and HTTPS pages are supported. Edge settings, local files, and other internal pages are excluded. This extension reads only the address you choose to send, not page content, cookies, or browser history. The reader fetches the page independently, so pages requiring your browser login may not work.
