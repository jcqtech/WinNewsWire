NetNewsWire
https://github.com/Ranchero-Software/NetNewsWire

WinNewsWire includes source ports and test fixtures from NetNewsWire.
The upstream reference for the current parity audit is
fb086eb7684e9fdb90855c4e033a6beba4325c5e.
